02 / OUR STORY & PURPOSE

Built on Technical Rigor & Zero Compromise

Founded in 2003 in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, M R Electrical Consultant was established to bridge the gap between complex power system theory and flawless on-site field execution.

Over the years, we have evolved into a comprehensive engineering partner for heavy industries, infrastructure developers, solar power producers, and government utilities. We manage the entire lifecycle: from initial load calculations and Single Line Diagrams (SLD), to CEIG / Discom liaisoning, protection relay testing, and final energized commissioning.

  • Licensed and chartered engineering consultancy with deep regulatory expertise in MP and across India.
  • Complete synchronization of electrical, civil, and structural works for EHV switchyards up to 220kV.
  • Full support for state electrical inspectorate (CEIG) approvals and utility grid connectivity.
  • Turnkey Solar EPC capabilities for industrial captive and ground-mounted solar power plants.

05 / HOW WE WORK

Our 4-Stage Methodology

A disciplined, transparent workflow ensuring timely delivery, optimal budget utilization, and zero regulatory roadblocks.

PHASE 01

Feasibility & Load Study

Site survey, soil resistivity testing, utility grid capacity analysis, connected load calculation, and preliminary SLD drafting.

PHASE 02

Detailed Engineering

Complete layout blueprints, short-circuit fault studies, transformer and breaker sizing, cable schedules, and protection schemes.

PHASE 03

Approvals & Procurement

CEIG drawing submission, DISCOM documentation, equipment inspection at manufacturer premises, and vendor coordination.

PHASE 04

Testing & Commissioning

Relay secondary injection testing, transformer oil breakdown voltage test, safety clearances verification, and official energized handover.
